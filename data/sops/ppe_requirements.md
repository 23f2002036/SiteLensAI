# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements — Construction Sites

## Mandatory PPE for All Workers

Every worker entering a construction site must wear the following minimum PPE at all times:

1. **Hard Hat (ANSI Z89.1 Class E or G)**
   - Must be worn at all times on the construction site
   - Replace immediately if cracked, dented, or damaged
   - Do not drill holes or modify the shell
   - Replace every 5 years or per manufacturer recommendation

2. **Safety Glasses (ANSI Z87.1)**
   - Impact-resistant lenses required for all site work
   - Side shields required in areas with flying debris
   - Prescription safety glasses must meet ANSI Z87.1 standards

3. **High-Visibility Vest (ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2 or 3)**
   - Required in all areas where vehicle or equipment traffic is present
   - Class 3 required for roadwork and low-visibility conditions
   - Must be clean and reflective strips must be visible

4. **Safety Boots (ASTM F2413)**
   - Steel-toe or composite-toe boots required
   - Puncture-resistant sole
   - Must be laced and in good condition
   - Electrical hazard (EH) rated boots required near electrical work

5. **Work Gloves**
   - Appropriate to the task being performed
   - Cut-resistant gloves for handling sharp materials
   - Chemical-resistant gloves for handling hazardous substances
   - Insulated gloves for electrical work

## Task-Specific PPE

### Welding Operations
- Welding helmet with appropriate shade lens (minimum Shade 10 for arc welding)
- Fire-resistant clothing
- Welding gloves (leather)
- Steel-toe boots

### Working at Heights
- Full body harness (ANSI Z359.11)
- Lanyard with shock absorber
- Self-retracting lifeline when applicable
- Hard hat with chin strap

### Concrete Work
- Rubber boots (waterproof)
- Chemical-resistant gloves
- Long-sleeve shirt to prevent concrete burns
- Safety glasses or goggles

### Demolition
- Full-face respirator (where dust/debris is present)
- Hearing protection (above 85 dB)
- Heavy-duty cut-resistant gloves
- Full body protection

## PPE Inspection Protocol

Before each shift:
1. Inspect all PPE for visible damage
2. Check harness webbing for cuts, fraying, or chemical damage
3. Verify hard hat shell integrity (no cracks or UV damage)
4. Ensure safety glasses lenses are clean and unscratched
5. Check boot soles for wear and puncture resistance
6. Report any damaged PPE to supervisor immediately
7. Do NOT use damaged PPE under any circumstances

## Non-Compliance Consequences

- First violation: Verbal warning and immediate correction
- Second violation: Written warning and mandatory safety retraining
- Third violation: Suspension from site pending safety review
- Willful violations: Immediate removal from site
