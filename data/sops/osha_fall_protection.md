# OSHA Fall Protection Standards — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M

## General Requirements (1926.501)

Each employee on a walking/working surface with an unprotected side or edge which is 6 feet (1.8 m) or more above a lower level shall be protected from falling by the use of guardrail systems, safety net systems, or personal fall arrest systems.

Workers on construction sites must be provided fall protection at elevations of 6 feet or more. This includes work on scaffolds, elevated platforms, rooftops, open-sided floors, and near excavations.

## Fall Protection Systems (1926.502)

### Guardrail Systems
- Top edge height: 42 inches (±3 inches) above walking/working level
- Midrails installed at a height midway between the top edge and walking/working surface
- Must withstand 200 pounds of force applied in any outward or downward direction

### Personal Fall Arrest Systems
- Full body harness connected to anchorage point
- Anchorage must support at least 5,000 pounds per worker
- Total fall distance plus deceleration distance must not exceed the available free fall distance
- Self-retracting lifelines must limit free fall to 2 feet or less
- Harness must be inspected before each use

### Safety Net Systems
- Installed as close as practicable under the walking/working surface
- Maximum distance below: 30 feet
- Must extend outward from the outermost projection of the work surface

## Training Requirements (1926.503)

Employers must provide training for each employee who might be exposed to fall hazards. Training must include:
1. Nature of fall hazards in the work area
2. Correct procedures for erecting, maintaining, disassembling, and inspecting fall protection systems
3. Use and operation of guardrail systems, personal fall arrest systems, safety net systems, and other protection methods
4. Role of each employee in the safety monitoring system

## Common Fall Hazards on Construction Sites

### Leading Edges
Workers at leading edges (the edge of a floor, roof, or formwork that changes location as additional sections are placed) must use fall protection.

### Holes and Openings
Floor holes through which a person can fall must be guarded by a cover or guardrail system. Wall openings from which there is a drop of more than 6 feet must be guarded.

### Scaffolding
Fall protection is required for scaffolds more than 10 feet above a lower level. Guardrails and/or personal fall arrest systems must be used.

### Roofing Work
Workers on low-slope roofs with unprotected sides and edges 6 feet or more above lower levels must have fall protection. Warning line systems may be used in combination with other systems.

## Emergency Response

In the event of a fall:
1. Activate emergency response immediately (call 911)
2. Do NOT move the fallen worker unless there is immediate danger
3. Provide first aid while waiting for emergency services
4. Secure the area to prevent additional falls
5. Report the incident to the site supervisor and safety officer
6. Document the incident including conditions, equipment used, and witnesses
