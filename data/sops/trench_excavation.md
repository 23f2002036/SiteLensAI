# Trench and Excavation Safety — Standard Operating Procedure

## Scope

This SOP applies to all excavation and trenching operations where workers may enter trenches or excavations deeper than 5 feet, or any depth where hazardous ground conditions exist.

## Hazards

- Cave-ins (the leading cause of fatality in trench operations)
- Falls into trenches
- Hazardous atmospheres (methane, hydrogen sulfide, low oxygen)
- Water accumulation and flooding
- Contact with underground utilities (gas, electric, water, sewer)
- Falling loads and equipment

## Pre-Excavation Requirements

### Utility Location
1. Contact the local utility locator service (811) at least 48 hours before digging
2. Hand-dig within 24 inches of marked utility locations
3. Protect exposed utilities from damage during excavation
4. Maintain safe distances from all marked utilities

### Soil Classification
A competent person must classify the soil before excavation begins:
- **Type A**: Most stable (cohesive soils like clay), 1.5H:1V slope
- **Type B**: Medium stability (silt, sandy loam), 1H:1V slope
- **Type C**: Least stable (gravel, sand, submerged soil), 1.5H:1V slope

### Protective Systems
Trenches 5 feet or deeper require one of the following:
1. **Sloping**: Cutting back the trench wall at an angle
2. **Shoring**: Installing support systems (hydraulic, timber, or mechanical)
3. **Shielding**: Using trench boxes or shields
4. **Benching**: Creating steps in the trench walls (not allowed in Type C soil)

## During Excavation

### Daily Inspection
A competent person must inspect the excavation:
- At the start of each shift
- After every rainstorm or water intrusion
- After any event that could affect soil stability
- Continuously during active excavation near workers

### Access and Egress
- Provide a ladder, ramp, or stairway in trenches 4 feet or deeper
- Access points must be within 25 feet of all workers
- Structural ramps must be designed by a qualified person

### Spoil Pile Management
- Keep spoil piles at least 2 feet from the trench edge
- Ensure no equipment or materials are placed near the edge
- Use barriers to prevent spoils from falling back into the trench

### Water Hazards
- Remove standing water before workers enter
- Use dewatering systems if water accumulates
- Workers must not enter flooded trenches
- Monitor weather conditions for potential flash flooding

## Emergency Procedures

### Cave-in or Collapse
1. Call 911 immediately
2. Do NOT enter the collapsed trench
3. Attempt to locate the buried worker without entering
4. Begin rescue operations only with proper equipment and training
5. Notify site supervisor and safety officer
6. Preserve the scene for investigation

### Hazardous Atmosphere
1. Evacuate the trench immediately
2. Do not re-enter without atmospheric testing
3. Ventilate the trench before re-entry
4. Use continuous atmospheric monitoring
5. Ensure rescue equipment is available before entry into deep trenches
