# Crane Operations Safety — Standard Operating Procedure

## Pre-Operation Inspection

Before any crane operation begins, the following checks must be completed:

1. **Daily Pre-Shift Inspection** (performed by qualified operator)
   - Inspect all wire ropes for kinking, crushing, bird-caging, or broken strands
   - Check hook, latch, and swivel for damage or deformation
   - Verify all safety devices are functional (anti-two-block, load moment indicator)
   - Inspect outriggers and stabilizers
   - Check hydraulic system for leaks
   - Verify boom condition and pin connections
   - Test all controls and emergency stops

2. **Ground Conditions**
   - Ensure ground can support crane and load weight
   - Use outrigger pads or mats on soft ground
   - Check for underground utilities before setup
   - Maintain level setup (max 1% grade)

## Operating Procedures

### Load Capacity
- Never exceed the rated capacity shown on the load chart
- Account for all load components: rigging weight, hook block, headache ball
- Reduce capacity for wind speeds above 20 mph
- Consider dynamic loading factors for sudden starts/stops

### Rigging Requirements
- Only qualified riggers shall rig loads
- Use tag lines to control load swing
- All slings must be inspected before each use
- Never wrap wire rope around a load — use proper slings
- Ensure proper sling angles (minimum 60° from horizontal)
- Use softeners at sharp edges to prevent sling damage

### Lift Zone Safety
- Establish a clear lift zone with barricades and signage
- No personnel under suspended loads at any time
- Signal person required when operator cannot see load
- Use standard hand signals or radio communication
- Crane operator has authority to stop any lift for safety concerns

### Overhead Power Lines
- Maintain minimum clearance of 20 feet from power lines up to 350 kV
- Contact utility company for de-energization if closer work is needed
- Use a dedicated spotter when working near power lines
- Ground the crane if contact is possible

## Emergency Procedures

### Load Drop or Crane Tip
1. Evacuate the area immediately
2. Call emergency services (911)
3. Do not attempt to re-right or recover the crane
4. Secure the area with a 100-foot perimeter
5. Report to safety officer and site supervisor

### Two-Block Condition
1. Stop all crane motion immediately
2. Lower the load block slowly if possible
3. Inspect wire rope and anti-two-block device
4. Do not resume operations until repaired and re-inspected

### High Wind Shutdown
- Cease operations when wind speed exceeds 30 mph
- Secure boom in lowest possible position
- Retract and secure the crane if wind exceeds 40 mph
- Monitor weather conditions continuously
