# Electrical Safety — Construction Site SOP

## Hazard Identification

Electrical hazards on construction sites include:
- Contact with overhead power lines
- Contact with energized equipment or circuits
- Damaged or improper wiring and extension cords
- Wet conditions increasing conductivity
- Lack of proper grounding
- Arc flash and arc blast

## General Electrical Safety Rules

1. Only qualified electricians shall work on electrical systems
2. Treat all wires as energized until verified by qualified person
3. Use lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures before any electrical work
4. Maintain safe distances from power lines (minimum 10 feet for up to 50 kV)
5. Use Ground Fault Circuit Interrupters (GFCIs) on all temporary wiring
6. Never use damaged cords, plugs, or receptacles
7. Keep electrical equipment away from water and wet conditions
8. Ensure proper grounding of all tools and equipment

## Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure

### Before Work Begins
1. Identify all energy sources that must be controlled
2. Notify all affected employees of the shutdown
3. Shut down the equipment using normal stopping procedure
4. Apply lockout/tagout devices to each energy-isolating device
5. Release or restrain all stored energy (capacitors, springs, hydraulics)
6. Verify zero energy state using appropriate test equipment
7. Perform the work

### Restoring Equipment
1. Inspect the work area to ensure all tools are removed
2. Verify all employees are clear of the equipment
3. Remove lockout/tagout devices (only by the person who applied them)
4. Re-energize equipment following proper sequence
5. Test equipment operation
6. Notify all affected employees that work is complete

## Emergency Response — Electrical Contact

### Worker Contact with Energized Source
1. DO NOT touch the victim if they are still in contact with the source
2. Call 911 immediately
3. Disconnect power source if safely possible
4. If power cannot be disconnected, use non-conductive material to separate victim from source
5. Begin CPR if victim is not breathing and has no pulse
6. Treat for shock — keep victim warm and comfortable
7. Do not move victim unless there is immediate danger

### Downed Power Line
1. Stay at least 35 feet away from downed lines
2. Assume all downed lines are energized
3. Call utility company and 911 immediately
4. Keep all personnel and equipment away
5. If a vehicle is in contact with a downed line, occupants should remain inside unless there is fire
6. If you must exit a vehicle near a downed line, jump clear without touching the vehicle and ground simultaneously

## Temporary Wiring Requirements

- All temporary wiring must be protected from damage
- Extension cords must be rated for the application
- Multi-plug adapters are not permitted
- Temporary lighting must be protected with guards
- All circuits must have GFCI protection
- Inspect all temporary wiring daily
